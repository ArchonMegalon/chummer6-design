# GHOSTWIRE

Tables can replay, inspect, and compare what happened without forking durable session state.

![GHOSTWIRE feature art](../assets/horizons/ghostwire.png)

## Why this matters

We know something went wrong, but we cannot reconstruct what actually happened.

Picture the scene: A GM replays a contested combat turn, sees receipts over time, and generates a grounded after-action recap.


## Does it work today?

Today: A first usable version works today.

Still improving: Make it richer, steadier, and easier to trust.
