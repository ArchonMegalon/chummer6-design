# ORIGIN DOSSIER

Guide fit: Part of the product story.

The player gets approved origin canon, dossier media, and later ALICE context without letting story prose rewrite the sheet.

![ORIGIN DOSSIER feature art](../assets/horizons/origin-dossier.png)

## How to use this

Use Origin Dossier when a legal sheet still feels unfinished as a person. It turns approved origin canon into contacts, debts, enemies, scars, secrets, portraits, narration, and media packets that the table can review.

The dossier can feed later ALICE context and optional player-scoped audiobook requests. It must not mutate the sheet, hand a desktop client global Audiobookshelf access, or let a render provider become character truth.

## Why this matters

A runner can start as math before they feel like a person.
