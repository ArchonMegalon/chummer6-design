# TABLE PULSE

GMs get a governed live heat-and-reaction path today and a separate private aftermath coaching path as the broader Table Pulse promise grows.

![TABLE PULSE feature art](../assets/horizons/table-pulse.png)

## How to use this

Use Table Pulse when the table needs live pressure without a surveillance dashboard. The GM sees bounded signals and can decide what becomes table action.

Private aftermath, remote reactions, quiet hours, and opt-outs are part of the feature, not paperwork around it.

## Why this matters

I need a live pressure path during play and a private coaching path after play, without confusing the two or turning either into surveillance.

Picture the scene: During a run, heat crosses a threshold and the GM receives a governed reaction packet; after the run, the GM can later open a private aftermath packet for pacing, spotlight, and confusion follow-through.


## Does it work today?

## Explanation video

[Watch the TABLE PULSE 90-second deep dive](https://chummer.run/media/horizons/table-pulse-90s-deepdive.mp4). [Captions](https://chummer.run/media/horizons/table-pulse-90s-deepdive.vtt).

Table Pulse has two paths. The design only works if those paths stay explicit.

**Table Pulse Live** is the GM-controlled heat and reaction path. During a session or in the
immediate aftermath of a session, security, matrix, magic, astral, faction, law, media, or public
pressure can cross a threshold. If the GM allows it, Chummer can notify eligible players or
faction contacts, let them play a bounded reaction mini-game, and return the result to the GM for
approval.

**Table Pulse Aftermath** is the private coaching path. After a session, the GM can review pacing,
spotlight balance, confusion points, disengagement markers, and follow-up suggestions without
turning Chummer into surveillance or player scoring.

The live path and the coaching path may share receipts, mute controls, and GM policy, but they are
not the same product promise and should not be described as if they are one heat system.
