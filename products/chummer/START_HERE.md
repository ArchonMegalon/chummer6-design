## Fast path by question

* What is Chummer6 today? -> `PUBLIC_LANDING_MANIFEST.yaml` and `PUBLIC_FEATURE_REGISTRY.yaml`
* Is it safe to try on my platform? -> `PUBLIC_RELEASE_EXPERIENCE.yaml` and `PUBLIC_DOWNLOADS_POLICY.md`
* What is actually shipped vs promised? -> `PUBLIC_HELP_COPY.md` and `FLAGSHIP_RELEASE_ACCEPTANCE.yaml`
* What does release governance enforce? -> `RELEASE_PIPELINE.md` and `WEEKLY_PRODUCT_PULSE.generated.json`
* Where do support and recovery start? -> `public-guide/HELP.md` and `PUBLIC_HELP_COPY.md`
* How do campaign and continuity lanes fit? -> `CAMPAIGN_SPINE_AND_CREW_MODEL.md` and `CHARACTER_LIFECYCLE_AND_LIVING_DOSSIER.md`
* What is still open vs done? -> `CAMPAIGN_OS_GAP_AND_CHANGE_GUIDE.md`

## Reading discipline

* Start with the smallest path that answers your question.
* If a page says "planned" or "future", route to a currently shipped page first.
* For review and claims, read evidence before any claim appears stable.
